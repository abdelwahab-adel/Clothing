Our Story

Great fashion, made easy to shop.

Founded in 2016 as a small shop in Downtown Cairo, Zay started with a simple idea: make good fashion easy to find and easy to buy. Today we are an online store bringing together top brands and everyday styles for women, men and kids.

Every order is checked and packed by our team before it ships, and delivered to your door across Egypt. We believe shopping should be simple: a wide selection, fair prices and friendly support when you need it.

  • Top brands and trending styles in one place
  • Hand-picked by our buying team
  • Every order checked before it ships
  • Loved by 120k+ happy customers
