Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

When you place an order or send us a message, the details you type — name, phone number, optional email, delivery address, city, postal code, country, payment method and the items you ordered — are put into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send to our store number. This website has no server or database of its own and does not store those details. WhatsApp (Meta) processes the message under its own terms and privacy policy, and we use what you send only to confirm, deliver and support your order.

  • Your cart and wishlist are kept only in your browser's local storage on your device; clearing it removes them.
  • To display the site, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts, some photos from Unsplash and the store map from OpenStreetMap. Like any web request, this shares your IP address and browser details with those providers.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising trackers on this site.
  • To ask us to access, correct or delete details you have sent us, message us on WhatsApp or email hello@zay.eg.

Shipping Information

Last updated: January 2026

Standard shipping is free on all orders over EGP 1,000; orders below that threshold ship for a flat EGP 60 courier rate. Typical delivery windows referenced across the site are 1–3 business days within Cairo & Giza, 2–5 business days to other governorates, and 7–14 business days for international orders.

  • Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of confirmation.
  • Tracking details would be emailed once an order ships, once this demo is connected to a real fulfilment backend.
  • Shipping costs and timelines shown throughout the site are illustrative for this design demo.

Returns & Exchanges

Last updated: January 2026

We want you to love what you order. Unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached are eligible for return or exchange within 30 days of delivery.

  • Items marked "Final Sale" are not eligible for return.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method once a return is received and inspected.
  • Exchanges for a different size or color are subject to availability.
